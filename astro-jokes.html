Theorists, experimentalists, and simulators

Q: What is the difference between theorists, experimentalists, and simulators?

A: Theorists are those people that nobody believes in their work, except for themselves.
Experimentalists are those people that everybody believes in their work, except for themselves.
Simulators are those people that nobody believes in their work, including themselves.

I’m not the original author of this one — credit goes to an unknown internet fella.

Dark matter vs. dark energy

This one is a true story. In my sophomore year I applied to a summer research internship in cosmology, and was interviewed with the question: what is the relation between dark matter and dark energy?

I knew basically nothing back then, but I didn’t want to sit there like a speechless idiot. So I made a well-educated guess: “I think they are the same thing.”

Yes, the committee professors were laughing out loud — like you right now.

After the laugh, they asked: why?

I knew I’d messed up, but I still had to reply — with my well-educated guess:

Because E = mc2.
