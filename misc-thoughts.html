A long-term daydream

Physics modelling of the “dynamics of history and sociology”, in the form of a video game.

Why this could be a good idea

Data acquisition

There would be so many degrees of freedom in trying to physically model human society — whether the complete way, from micro human interactions to macro social structures, or the phenomenological approach — that scanning the parameter space would be a nightmare of computational time. But make it a video game — let’s say a successful one — and players are basically providing free simulations.

Propagation

The engagement of more people is itself meaningful — whatever choices they make in the game, the participation matters. This project aims to understand how human society evolves into the state we have today, the starting point from which we can think together about where we go next — especially in the fast-changing era of AI. It could be an effort to make the future decided by the many, not the few. Consensus is good, but even without it, understanding ourselves and each other is also good.

Sustainability

Selling game copies can raise funds to make the development self-sustaining, especially for this kind of weird combination of fields (maybe it’s not even legit to call it inter-disciplinary).

Foreseeable difficulties

  • Compared to cosmology and large-scale structure, there is probably no good first-principles starting point — nothing like Newtonian physics to build on.
  • The “observables” are still poorly defined — what metrics should we use to trace this social-dynamical evolution, and are they natural or fine-tuned?
  • We only have one realization — the original timeline — so we don’t know how much of an outlier we are sitting at, even given the same “primitive society” as an initial condition.

A few early stage questions one can easily ask

  • Which aspects of our contemporary society are coincidental, and which are “pre-destined”?
  • Is there a robust “large-scale structure”?
  • Among these dynamical attractors in the parameter space, were there “better” social paths we missed in real history (the original timeline), and “worse” ones we dodged?
  • What possible futures lie in front of us now — which do we want, and which do we want to avoid? How?
